<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

In art and science, minds spark bright,  
The doers and thinkers unite,  
They create wonders day and night.

There’s a secret, a hidden power,  
Those who create, think and act in one hour,  
They gaze into the future with a clear sight,  
And bring things to life, up and to the right.

Let us embrace this with passion and drive,  
To be the masters of our fate and thrive.  
For in the fusion of thought and deed,  
We shall rise and succeed, indeed.
