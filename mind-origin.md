<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

Water seeks the ocean,  
A journey of return,  
From drop to vast expanse,  
A homecoming to discern.

The mind, like water, yearns,  
For its origin to find,  
Through life’s trials and turns,  
A quest of heart and mind.

In the ocean’s depths,  
Water finds its peace,  
In the mind’s still center,  
A heart at peace, at ease.

As water flows to sea,  
The mind flows to its source,  
In the eternal mystery,  
A dance of life within.

Water seeks the ocean,  
The mind seeks its origin,  
In the cosmic ocean’s motion,  
A dance of life within.
