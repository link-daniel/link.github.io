<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

In the depths of shadow and despair,  
A prisoner emerged from the dungeon’s lair,  
With strength and courage, he took flight,  
Breaking free from the chains of his plight.

From the ashes of defeat and pain,  
He rose again, his spirit unchained,  
A beacon of light in the darkest hour,  
A symbol of hope, a mighty power.

With every step, he climbed higher,  
Leaving behind the darkness and the mire,  
His eyes fixed on the distant light,  
A vision of a future night.

From the depths of his struggle,  
He found the strength to rise,  
No longer bound by his past,  
He embraced a new sunrise.
