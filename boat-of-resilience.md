<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

In the boat, we row,  
Down the stream, we go,  
A journey through the night,  
A struggle in the light.

The water’s cold, the current’s strong,  
A battle we must face, along,  
A darkness in the dream,  
A shadow in the stream.

In the boat, we find our place,  
A refuge from the chase,  
An emblem of our tenacity,  
In the face of all adversity.

So row, row, row the boat,  
Through the stream, we float,  
A haven in the storm,  
A dream, a different norm.
