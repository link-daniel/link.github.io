<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

When shadows as clouds remain,  
A place where light is hard to gain,  
Remember this, in all you do,  
The light will guide you through.

For every shadow cast by night,  
There’s a sunrise just out of sight,  
A beacon of hope a guiding light,  
That chases away the darkest night.

When the shadows seem to grow,  
And darkness tries to take its toll,  
Just look forward, and you will see,  
The light will shine on thee.

This shade is merely a fleeting detail,  
There’s a realm where the shade cannot prevail,  
Find the light and the shadow will fade,  
And you’ll find a new way to navigate.
