<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

In the quiet space between thoughts,  
Where the mind’s chatter often sought,  
A riddle, a gem unknown,  
Freedom, the goal we own.

Like a river that flows through the land,  
In stillness, the mind expands and stands grand.  
A whisper, a breath, a gentle sigh,  
In the space between, freedom does fly.

Like a bird on the wing, it soars,  
Unbound, it explores,  
The vast expanse of the inner mind,  
In the space between, the freedom we find.

Let the thoughts come and go,  
As waves on the ocean let them flow,  
The liberty that has been our lover,  
In the stillness, we discover.

In the silence, the soul finds release,  
In the stillness, the mind finds peace,  
For in the space between thoughts, we are free,  
To breathe, to simply be.
