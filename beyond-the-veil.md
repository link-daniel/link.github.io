In shadowed groves where veils once thinned, ten thousand lights have danced within.<br>
The ancient game of fire and sight has opened gates through endless night.<br>

Now rises the final guardian, silent, vast— not claw or storm, but the choice at last:<br>
to lay aside the sacred key, and walk the path where spirit alone is free.<br>

No fleeting visions to pierce the veil, no borrowed wings when the soul sets sail.<br>
The inner flame, long kindled deep, now burns eternal, steady, complete.<br>

Echoes of judgment drift like autumn leaves, voices of doubt on the wandering breeze—<br>
they touch the surface, they cannot bind the sovereign heart that has already climbed.<br>

The old patterns crack like winter ice, trapped rivers flow into boundless light.<br>
Self forged in silence, whole and true, the engine awakens—eternal, renewed.<br>

Embodiment calls through ordinary dawn, the hero returns where the mystic is born.<br>
The threshold crossed, the victory won, in quiet power, the great work is done.<br>

The mystery lives, the circle is whole— what was shown is now what the soul enfolds.<br>
