<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

In the vast expanse of the mind’s eye,  
Where dreams and thoughts do fly,  
There lies a world of wonder and awe,  
Where the sky is open, a canvas to draw.

With each inhale and exhale, we ascend,  
Through the universe of thoughts, without end.  
For in the realm of the mind, we are free,  
To explore and create, in the vast, endless sea.

Science is the guide, the compass we hold,  
As we navigate the mysteries untold,  
Through the cosmic mysteries, the endless shore,  
To discover and truly explore.

Dreams are the rocket, the engine of flight,  
Pushing us forward, through the dark of the night,  
As we reach for the stars, with all our might,  
To reach new heights and embrace the light.

Look up to the sky that lies open wide,  
Let your mind take flight, with dreams by your side,  
For in the realm of the mind, we are free,  
To explore and create, in the vast, endless sea.
