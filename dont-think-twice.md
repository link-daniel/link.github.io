<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

As the sun sets and the night draws near,  
I whisper goodbye, with no trace of fear.  
The memories we shared, once bright and clear,  
Fade into the past, as if never here.

No need to dwell, no need to cry,  
The winds of change, they never lie.  
Each day a new dawn, a chance to try,  
To live and love, beneath the open sky.

So don’t think twice, it’s alright,  
Our paths diverge, in the light of the night.  
The road ahead, it beckons bright,  
With dreams to chase, and stars to ignite.

Farewell to yesterday, with no regret,  
A new beginning, we won’t forget.  
The love we shared, it will always stay,  
In the gentle whispers of a brand new day.
