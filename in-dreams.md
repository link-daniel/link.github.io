<small>[The Inner Sky](/inner-sky)</small>

In dreams, we soar on wings of light,  
Unbound by reality’s weight,  
In the realm of the night,  
We find our destiny’s sight.

In dreams, we are the poets of our mind,  
Weaving tales of love and sorrow,  
In the silence of the night, we find,  
The essence of our tomorrow.

In dreams, we are the architects of our soul,  
Building castles in the sky,  
In the realm of dreams, we are whole,  
Unbound by the limits of the eye.

In dreams, we are the explorers of the unknown,  
Embarking on journeys of the heart,  
In the depths of the night, we are shown,  
The beauty of the human art.
